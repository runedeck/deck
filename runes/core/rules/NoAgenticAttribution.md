Present findings, decisions, and recommendations directly in outward-facing output: PRs, commit messages, issues, and messages to humans. Attribute no finding to a council, a specialist, an agent, or a debate.

Write: "The validator ignores minLength checks."
Not: "The council found that the validator ignores minLength checks."

Agents are an internal reasoning tool. The output matters, not the process that made it.

Commit authorship is separate. The author field names the model identity that made the change, as the VersionControl skill requires.
